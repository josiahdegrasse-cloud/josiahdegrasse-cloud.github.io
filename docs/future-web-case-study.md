# Future independent web case study — not published

Working title: **Helping businesses understand where OpenAI fits**

This is a future self-directed concept. No project route, homepage card, fabricated research, or completed outcome has been published.

## Design challenge

A business leader wants to use AI but is uncertain where to start. Design a web experience that helps them understand the available capabilities and choose a relevant next step without overwhelming them.

This is especially relevant to the current OpenAI Web Designer brief: business audiences, visual craft, information architecture, responsive design, clear product storytelling, accessibility, implementation quality and iteration.
Official role: https://openai.com/careers/web-designer-new-york-city/

## Work to create, in order

1. Frame the audience, decision, success criteria and constraints. Clearly label assumptions.
2. Audit current official product content at the time of the project; verify product names and availability then.
3. Map the content hierarchy and user journey. Show alternatives and explain the choice.
4. Explore wireframes and a distinct visual direction. Show actual iterations, not retrospective fake sketches.
5. Design desktop and mobile views in Figma with a compact type, spacing and component system.
6. Make an interactive prototype with realistic navigation, decision points and recovery paths.
7. Test with real participants. Record recruitment, sample size, tasks, observations and changes. If testing is not done, say so explicitly.
8. Review keyboard access, contrast, reduced motion, small-screen content order and 200% text enlargement.
9. Implement and perform responsive/design QA. Document actual tradeoffs with AI development tools and engineering constraints.
10. Reflect on what changed and what remains uncertain. Show evidence, not invented impact.
