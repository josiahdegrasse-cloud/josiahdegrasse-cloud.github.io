## Prioritized missing evidence

1. **Red Hat original Figma work:** both divergent concepts, final high-fidelity screens, and review/rollback detail. Export at 2x, ideally at least 1600 pixels wide. Include a link to a viewable original prototype if shareable. Current placeholders live in `design-case-studies.tsx`.
2. **NFI research documentation:** participant count, tested tasks, think-aloud/session notes if used, improvement count, observations and revisions. The 100% task completion result was confirmed by Josiah, but the sample size and improvement count were not supplied. Do not infer them from the synthetic panel counts in product screenshots.
3. **NFI matched before/after:** the same task and screen in the earlier and revised product, with dates and 1–2 clear annotations explaining the change. Neither screenshot is invented.
4. **Original research artifacts:** Red Hat journey map, interview synthesis, opportunity definitions, validation feedback and leadership review. Current diagrams are explicitly labelled explanatory reconstructions.
5. **Original NFI exploration:** sketches, early wireframes, discarded alternatives and rationale. Prefer a small number of informative artifacts over a wall of thumbnails.
6. **Mobile product evidence:** genuine panelist/study flows from the sensory product and relevant responsive Figma exports. The portfolio itself was tested at 320, 375, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels. No resized desktop capture is represented as a mobile design.
7. **Secondary projects:** HeadTap real interface, Helfrich fixtures/drawings, lacrosse fabrication and test documentation. Existing CAD imagery is labelled as a study, without unverified manufacturing or test claims.
8. **Optional portrait:** a good natural portrait is still absent; the supplied lacrosse photo adds genuine personal context.

## Replacing an asset

Save the optimized file under its project folder. Replace the `AssetPlaceholder` with `ImageFigure` using an accurate alt, source/context caption, and the actual width/height (default 1280×720). Use original artifacts only. Keep layout dimensions stable and below-the-fold images lazy-loaded. The enlarged-image dialog provides a scrollable original-resolution view on small screens.
